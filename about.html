Fireworks Safety Guidelines

  • Always read instructions on the label before use.
  • Keep children at a safe distance; never let them handle fireworks unsupervised.
  • Light one firework at a time and move away quickly.
  • Never attempt to relight a firework that did not ignite properly.
  • Keep water or sand nearby to extinguish used fireworks.
  • Never carry fireworks in your pocket or throw them at people.
  • Use crackers only in open, outdoor areas away from trees and flammable material.
  • Follow local government timings for using fireworks during Diwali.
  • Be considerate of elderly neighbours, children, and animals — keep noise to reasonable levels.
  • Dispose of used fireworks carefully after soaking in water.

Legal & Licensing

Sivakasi666crackers operates under a valid retail fireworks license issued under The Explosives Act, 1884 and The Explosives Rules, 2008. All products sold comply with BIS standards (IS:7883) and bear the mandatory safety certifications.

Sale of fireworks is subject to local municipal and state government regulations. Customers are responsible for following applicable laws in their area. Sivakasi666crackers does not deliver to areas where fireworks are prohibited. By placing an order, you confirm you are 18 years of age or older.

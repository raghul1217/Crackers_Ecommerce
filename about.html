Safety Guidelines

  • Read label instructions before use
  • Keep children at safe distance, supervised
  • Light one firework at a time, move away quickly
  • Never relight a misfired firework
  • Keep water or sand nearby
  • Use only in open outdoor areas
  • Follow Diwali government timings
  • Soak and dispose used fireworks safely
